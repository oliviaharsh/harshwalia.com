Risk Management Implications
🏗️
LO1, Core Structure
Economic Benefits
Originators transfer credit risk off-balance-sheet via SPV. Illiquid 25-year mortgages become tradeable ABS. Widens investor base, reduces funding cost for banks.
⚡
LO2, Risk Transfer
Credit, Liquidity & Market Risk
Credit risk redistributed by tranching. Liquidity risk reduced for originator. Market risk remains for investors via spread widening. Operational risk in SPV servicing.
🏦
LO3, Strategic
Capital Efficiency & Basel III
Reduces risk-weighted assets. Freeing regulatory capital enables new lending. ~8% RWA reduction per £1M securitised. Improves balance sheet efficiency materially.
⚠️
LO2, Key Limitation
Information Asymmetry
Originator knows more than investors (lemon problem). "Originate-to-distribute" removes underwriting incentives. Rating agencies systematically mispriced risk pre-2008.
📉
LO1, Limitation
Correlation & Systemic Risk
Defaults cluster in downturns. AAA tranches priced on flawed independence assumptions. In 2008, 28%+ default rates impaired even senior tranches, destroying investor confidence.
